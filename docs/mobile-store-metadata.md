# Beta store metadata draft — owner review before submission

Name: Bodyweight Ladder
Feedback/support: hello@ashbi.ca
Website: https://ladder.ashbi.ca/

Beta description: Build a bodyweight routine at your pace. Choose your equipment and available time, follow exercise ladders, record sets, and review saved progress. This beta uses device-local storage and manual export/restore. Generated exercise images are illustrative and await professional technique review.

What to Test: Exercise setup, starting and pausing holds, rest adjustments, skip/swap/finish, background/resume, backup export and restore, offline launch, accessibility and layout on your device. Please report the screen and steps; do not email health information or progress backups.

Privacy disclosure draft: The app does not add analytics, advertising, tracking or automatic workout uploads. Workout records and preferences stay in app storage. Export is user-initiated and shares only with the destination selected in the OS share sheet. Reports open an email draft; sending is voluntary. External exercise resources operate under their own privacy policies. TestFlight diagnostics are handled by Apple. No account is required. Contact hello@ashbi.ca for support. Uninstalling or clearing data may remove local progress; exported files remain wherever you save them.

Before submission: approve and publish a stable privacy-policy URL, confirm developer identity/contact, check final SDK privacy report, complete Apple's App Privacy and Google's Data Safety/health-app declarations, answer age-rating and export-compliance questions, and supply actual-device screenshots. No declarations or agreements have been submitted on your behalf.
