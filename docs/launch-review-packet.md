# Human review and pilot packet

Status: prepared; no reviewer appointed, sign-off obtained or pilot participants enrolled. Do not describe this app as clinically validated, injury-aware or professionally approved.

## Exercise-professional review

Review the exact release recorded in Git alongside `coaching-engine.md`, `ui/coaching-engine.js`, the exercise definitions and instructions in `index.html`, and the generated `exercise-photo-review.html` contact sheet. The sheet lists every current technique-photo mapping and whether the referenced file is generated or licensed. Full licensed-source information is in `assets/brand/exercises/photo-sources.json` and the app's Photo credits dialog. Image generation and licenses do not establish correct technique.

For every ladder, record: exercise/step, cue accuracy, photo accuracy, proposed correction, appropriateness of equipment/supports, progression ordering, suggested range/sets/rest, and reviewer name/date/qualification. Review both sides of unilateral movements and whether the image alone could mislead. Pay particular attention to unsupported furniture, dips, table rows, hangs, weighted carries, swings, jumping and tendon-loading movements. Mark a step as approved, needs change, or outside the reviewer's scope. No blank row counts as approval.

For the engine, review: the movement relationship map; spacing intervals; weekly balancing coefficients; repeat/advance/regress suggestions; missing-effort handling; short and low-energy adjustments; return after breaks; and the minimum workload still offered when nothing fits the time budget. Separate evidence-supported principles from product heuristics. Confirm the app's actual target population and where users should seek qualified individual guidance. The app currently cannot assess injury, pregnancy, rehabilitation needs, medical fitness or measured recovery. Do not add personalized claims for those populations without a separately designed and reviewed flow.

## One-week pilot plan — not yet run

Recruit 15–20 consenting adults with varied experience, time available, equipment, screen sizes, comfort with technology, text-size settings and accessibility needs. Include beginners and people returning after a break. Do not recruit medical/rehabilitation populations as if this were a clinical study or imply safe suitability before professional review.

Use a short introduction: “Try a workout that feels appropriate to you. You can change, skip or stop an exercise. We are reviewing the product's clarity and reliability; we are not assessing your fitness.” Participation is optional. Do not ask for diagnoses, body photos or identifiable progress backups in public issues. Obtain separate consent before recordings or screen sharing.

Tasks to observe without coaching the interface:

1. Set a goal, choose equipment and explore the proposed exercise steps before starting.
2. Find why a workout was chosen and change its time or energy setting.
3. Complete, skip and swap exercises; understand the effort choice and next-step suggestion.
4. Leave and resume an unfinished session; find completed progress afterward.
5. Export completed progress, restore on another browser, and understand that unfinished sets do not transfer.
6. Find Report a problem, review what would be shared and identify that the destination is public.

Capture with anonymous participant IDs: task completion without help, confusing labels/controls, accidental actions, save concerns, perceived usefulness of explanations, and whether they would voluntarily return. Record browser/device only with permission. Keep private feedback in an owner-controlled location, not in Git. An observed pain/safety concern or progress-loss report takes priority over visual polish.

Suggested release decision after the pilot: resolve every progress-loss or safety-critical issue; fix any task repeatedly requiring help; confirm the reviewer sign-offs and an owner who receives uptime failures; then decide whether a wider launch is appropriate. These are proposed acceptance criteria, not completed results.
