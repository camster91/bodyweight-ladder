Bodyweight Ladder

Beta support

For help with setup, workouts, backup and restore, email hello@ashbi.ca.

Helpful details to include

  • App version and device model.
  • The screen and steps that led to the problem.
  • Whether the device was offline.
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain private progress or health information.

Do not email health information or progress backups. The app saves progress locally and does not send a report automatically; an email is sent only if you choose to send it.
