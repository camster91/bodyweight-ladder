# Third-party icon sources

The filled arm, leg and foot silhouettes used within the movement-symbol set are adapted from [Health Icons](https://healthicons.org/), hosted by Resolve to Save Lives.

Health Icons publishes its icons under CC0 (public domain); editing and commercial use are permitted. See [the project's license information](https://healthicons.org/about).

Source SVGs:

- https://github.com/resolvetosavelives/healthicons/blob/main/public/icons/svg/filled/body/arm.svg
- https://github.com/resolvetosavelives/healthicons/blob/main/public/icons/svg/filled/body/leg.svg
- https://github.com/resolvetosavelives/healthicons/blob/main/public/icons/svg/filled/body/foot.svg

The remaining equipment, torso, contact and mobility symbols were authored for Bodyweight Ladder. These compact symbols identify exercise families; step-specific technique illustrations and written cues provide the instructions.
