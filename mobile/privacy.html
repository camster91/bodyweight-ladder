Privacy in this beta

Workout history and preferences stay in this app’s storage. They do not automatically sync with the website or another device. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove them.

Backups are shared only when you choose Export and select a destination. A temporary export copy stays in app cache while the receiving app copies it. Save a durable copy somewhere you trust.

No advertising, analytics or automatic workout upload is added by this app. External demonstration pages have their own privacy policies. Problem reports open an email draft that you choose whether to send. Apple may process TestFlight diagnostics separately.

Contact: hello@ashbi.ca. Generated photos and exercise guidance await professional review.
