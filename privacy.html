Bodyweight Ladder

Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026.

What stays on your device

Bodyweight Ladder does not require an account. Workout records, exercise levels, routines, preferences and unfinished sessions are kept in the app's local storage. The app does not automatically upload workout records or sync them to another device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can remove local progress.

Backups and sharing

Backup export is user-initiated. When you choose an export or share action, the operating system presents its share or save sheet and you choose the destination. The app does not send a backup automatically. A saved backup remains wherever you choose to store it, and you should keep it private. Restore parses the selected file on the device and applies it only after you confirm the merge.

Support reports

Report a problem opens an email draft addressed to hello@ashbi.ca. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send a report, the email provider and the information you include will process it. Do not include health information or progress backups in a report.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The beta does not add analytics, advertising, tracking, or advertising identifiers. No account, advertising SDK or analytics SDK is included.

External resources

Optional exercise demonstrations and other external links open outside the app or use their own services. Those providers' privacy policies apply to activity on their services.

Platform diagnostics

Apple and Google may process diagnostics and app-store activity under their own policies. Bodyweight Ladder does not control those platform services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@ashbi.ca.
